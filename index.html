How it works

Emotions come in waves. We measure the water, not the weather.

Every community has a resting emotional state. We measure against it.

How high

Amplitude above baseline. A hot community hitting a new peak is noise. A quiet one spiking is a wave forming.

How fast

Rate of change. Slow climb: cultural drift. Vertical spike: breaking event.

How often

Recurrence. Increasing frequency means the baseline itself is shifting. The tide's coming in.

Together, they tell you how close a narrative is to becoming self-sustaining.

Tracked across three tiers — Leadership, Institutional Media, Grassroots — because where a signal starts and where it travels tells you as much as the signal itself.
